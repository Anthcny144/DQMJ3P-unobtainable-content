# DQMJ3P - Unobtainable content
This 3GX plugin allows unlocking content locked behind [discontinued features](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/63227/~/announcement-of-discontinuation-of-online-services-for-nintendo-3ds-and-wii-u) including online play, StreetPass and SpotPass, as well as monster transfers, for both DQMJ3 and DQMJ3P.<br>

## Compatibility
- ✅ **DQMJ3 1.0**<br>
- ✅ **DQMJ3 1.1**<br>
- ✅ **DQMJ3P 1.0**<br>
- ✅ **DQMJ3P 1.3**<br>
This should be compatible with every untranslated and translated version as long as the game's code is untouched.

# Installing on 3DS
- Insert your SD card into your computer
- Download the zip archive in [Releases](https://github.com/Anthcny144/DQMJ3P-unobtainable-monsters/releases)
- <b>Extract</b> the content of the zip file into your SD card (replace files if asked to)
- Eject your SD card and plug it back in your 3DS
- Turn on your 3DS and press `L + Down + Select` on the Home Menu
- Toggle on "Plugin Loader"
- Start the game. On the title screen, press Select to open the menu<br>
If you don't see a blue flash upon launching the game, that means you did something wrong during the installation. Make sure you don't have multiple .3gx files at `/luma/plugins/00040000001ACB00/` or `/luma/plugins/000400000016AD00`.

# Installing on emulator
> [!NOTE]
> Use the [Azahar](https://azahar-emu.org/) emulator. The other ones are now obsolete and may not support 3GX plugins.<br>
- Launch Azahar
- On the top left corner, press "File", then "Open Azahar Files"
- Create a folder named `sdmc` if it does not exist
- Download the zip archive in [Releases](https://github.com/Anthcny144/DQMJ3P-unobtainable-monsters/releases)
- <b>Extract</b> the content of the zip file in the `sdmc` folder, then you can close this window
- On Azahar, on the top left corner, click on "Emulation", then "Configure"
- Go in the "System", scroll down and tick "Enable 3GX plugin loader", then click "OK"
- Start the game. On the title screen, press Select to open the menu<br>
If you don't see a blue flash upon launching the game, that means you did something wrong during the installation. Make sure you don't have multiple .3gx files at `/luma/plugins/00040000001ACB00/` or `/luma/plugins/000400000016AD00`.<br><br>
